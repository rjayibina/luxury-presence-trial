About Marci

Marci J. Metzger

REALTOR for nearly 3 decades

Marci built her career as a REALTOR, and later a licensed broker, in Washington State. These days she's trading rain for sunshine, helping buyers and sellers settle into Southern Nevada. She's been guiding people through the ups and downs of real estate since 1995 — through shifting markets, growing families, and second chapters — and brings all of that experience to every client she works with today.

Marci lives in the Mountain Falls community herself, and she'll work just as hard to find a place that fits you the way it fits her.

“I love that small-town feeling our community offers — spectacular golf courses, parks, a pool, and easy access to Las Vegas make Pahrump a great place to call home. Working or retired, fast-paced or looking to relax, there's a place for you here.”
